# License FAQ

### Can you summarize what CI-Agent-Sync's license allows?

The license grants free rights to use, modify, and redistribute CI-Agent-Sync source code for personal or nonprofit purposes. The intent is to stay permissive for those uses while limiting commercial abuse of Companion Intelligence products.

CI-Agent-Sync is the portable Companion Intelligence agent control plane.

CI-Agent-Sync uses the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0). The full text is in [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE).

### What freedoms do you have as a CI-Agent-Sync user?

If you use CI-Agent-Sync for personal or nonprofit purposes, you can view, modify, and redistribute the source under the same license terms, subject to the PolyForm Noncommercial restrictions.

### Can I fork CI-Agent-Sync?

Yes. For noncommercial use, you can fork, modify, and redistribute CI-Agent-Sync under the same license.

### Can I contribute to CI-Agent-Sync?

Yes. Open a pull request on this repository.

### Can I sell CI-Agent-Sync?

No. Selling CI-Agent-Sync, including paid access to a hosted instance, is not permitted. For commercial partnership questions, email partner@companionintelligence.com.

### Does this license apply to the rest of Companion Intelligence?

No. Companion Portal stays a separate commercial product. Most other Companion Intelligence product repositories use the same PolyForm Noncommercial terms in their own repositories — see the org's open-source rollout tracker for the current list.
